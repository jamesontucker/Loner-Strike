Arguments is the rest of the line ("/give 5": 5).eventPlayer SaidRuns when a player chats (anything; Team Only for team chat).eventFreeze Time EndedRuns when the freeze time ends and players can move.eventMap EndingRuns when the map ends (the scoreboard shows), just before the next map.eventWeapon FiredRuns when a player shoots (at most 10 times a second per player).eventReloadedRuns when a player starts reloading.eventHostage RescuedRuns when a player rescues a hostage.eventVIP EscapedRuns when the VIP escapes.eventPlayer About To Be HurtRuns just before a player takes damage.eventPlayer RevivedRuns when a downed player is brought back.eventDowned Player OutRuns when nobody revived a downed player in time.eventRound Time UpRuns when the mode's round clock reaches 0.eventCustom EventRuns when something calls this Custom Event (Call Custom Event, Set Timer by Event, or the map: an ls_event entity, or an info_target named lsev_<its map name>, when triggered).eventPlayer Pressed ButtonRuns when a player presses (or lets go of) a button the game knows: fire, right click, use, jump, duck, reload, walk or a move key.eventPlayer CommandRuns when a player uses a console command the mode names (ls_ and letters, like ls_dash): they can bind any key to it (bind f "ls_dash").eventPicked UpRuns when a player picks a weapon or an item up from the ground (not when they buy one or the mode gives it).eventDroppedRuns when a player drops a weapon (G, or the mode makes them).eventBegin OverlapRuns when something its filter counts goes into a trigger volume (Spawn Trigger Volume).eventEnd OverlapRuns when something leaves a trigger volume (or the volume is removed, or it died or left the server inside).eventEntity TouchedRuns when an entity touches another (props, projectiles, NPCs, creatures, players; not the world's walls and floors).eventBullet HitRuns where a shot lands: the shooter, the hit position, the surface's normal, what it hit (an entity, or World), the weapon and, on a player, where (head, chest...).eventPlayer LandedRuns when a player lands after a fall: how fast they were falling (units a second; over 580 hurts) and the fall damage the game gives for it (0 if Fall Damage Off).eventGrenade ThrownRuns when a player throws a grenade: who, the grenade (an entity: Set Grenade Fuse, Push, Remove Entity work on it), which kind and where.eventGrenade ExplodedRuns when a thrown grenade goes off (a smoke grenade when it starts smoking): who threw it, the grenade, which kind and where.eventOn Map Entity FiredRuns when a named entity of the map is triggered (by a button, a trigger, a timer or another entity) or used: its name, the entity, who set it off (a player, or nobody) and the entity that fired it.eventWeapon SwitchedRuns when a player takes out a weapon (a switch, a pick-up that goes to hand, a spawn): the player, the new weapon and the one before (short names: ak47, knife; empty when there was none), and the weapon entity.eventEntered Map ZoneRuns when a living player walks into one of the map's zones (checked four times a second).eventLeft Map ZoneRuns when a living player walks out of one of the map's zones (checked four times a second).eventOn TickRuns again and again, every Interval seconds (0.1: ten times a second; 0: as often as the mode's Limits setting lets it, 100 a second at first), all through the map.

    Round Started

    eventevent.round_started
    Round Started

      Runs when a round begins: freeze time is over and everyone can move.

      Outputs (1)
      Then Flow
      Goes on when it's done.
      Notes (1)

      The usual place to set a round up: give weapons, reset scores, show a message. It runs once per round for the whole server, not per player (use For Each Player for that).

      Related:New RoundFreeze Time EndedFor Each Player

        Player Killed

        eventevent.player_killed
        Player Killed
          • Victim
          • Attacker
          • Headshot
          • Weapon
          • Distance
          • Team kill
          • Suicide
          • Killed by the world
          • Weapon kind

          Runs when a player dies. Attacker is nobody when the world killed them (a fall, the bomb).

          Outputs (10)
          Then Flow
          Goes on when it's done.
          Victim Player
          Who died.
          Attacker Player
          Who killed them; nobody for the world, and the victim themselves for a suicide.
          Headshot Yes/No
          Yes when the killing shot hit the head.
          Weapon Text
          The weapon's short name, as the kill feed shows it: ak47, knife, grenade, world.
          Distance Number
          How far apart they were, in units (a player is 72 units tall).
          Team kill Yes/No
          Yes when the attacker shot a teammate.
          Suicide Yes/No
          Yes when the victim killed themselves (a grenade, the Kill node, the kill command).
          Killed by the world Yes/No
          Yes when no player did it.
          Weapon kind Weapon
          Weapon kind: a weapon (ak47, awp, knife...). Values: none, ak47, m4a1, awp, scout, famas, galil, aug, sg552, mp5navy, p90, ump45, mac10, tmp, m3, xm1014, m249, deagle, usp, glock18, p228, fiveseven, elite, hegrenade, flashbang, smokegrenade, knife, c4, g3sg1, sg550.
          Notes (1)

          Runs once for every death. Attacker is nobody when the world did it (a fall, the bomb, a trigger_hurt); check with Is Valid before giving the attacker anything.

          Related:Player HurtAdd ScoreRespawn

                                                            Player Command

                                                            eventin.command
                                                            Player Command
                                                              • Player
                                                              • Arguments

                                                              Runs when a player uses a console command the mode names (ls_ and letters, like ls_dash): they can bind any key to it (bind f "ls_dash"). Arguments is the rest of the line, as text only: it's never run. Each player can use it at most a few times a second (Player Command uses a second, in the mode's Limits settings).

                                                              Outputs (3)
                                                              Then Flow
                                                              Goes on when it's done.
                                                              Player Player
                                                              Who used the command.
                                                              Arguments Text
                                                              The rest of the line, as text (empty when there was none).
                                                              Settings (1)
                                                              Command (ls_...)
                                                              A name, as the map or the game knows it (letters, digits, _ . -). Default: ls_dash.
                                                              Limits (1)
                                                              Player Command uses a second
                                                              5 by default; hosts can raise it to 1000. Above 20 the server may slow down.
                                                              Notes (2)

                                                              A key the game has no button for (F, G, a mouse button) can still start a flow: name a command (ls_ and letters, like ls_dash), and players bind a key to it in their console (bind f "ls_dash"); Tell Player To Bind shows them the line. Arguments is whatever came after the command, as text only: the mode can read it, but it's never run as a command. Player Command uses a second (each player) keeps a held or scripted key from flooding the server: a setting in the mode's Limits group, 5 to start (the checks warn above 20).

                                                              Careful: Safe by design: the command name can only be ls_ and letters (never one of the game's own), and the arguments are never run.
