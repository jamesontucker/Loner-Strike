    Give Healing Item

    actionbr.heal_give
    Give Healing Item
    • Player
    • How many

    Gives a player bandages (+15 up to 75), medkits (full health) or drinks (+40 over 10 s), up to the carry limit.

    Inputs (2)
    Run Flow
    Runs this node.
    Player Player
    The player. Needs a wire.
    How many Number
    How many: a number. Leave it empty to use the setting.
    Settings (2)
    Item
    Pick one: Bandage, Medkit, Drink. Default: bandage.
    How many
    A number from 1 to 10. A wire into the pin of the same name is used instead. Default: 1.
    Notes (1)

    Needs Loner-Strike's components plugin, which comes with the app.

    Related:Healing ItemsSet Armor LevelsMake Crate

    • At
    • Crate

    Crate Entity
    Crate: an entity (a thing in the map).

    Match HUD Words

    actionbr.hud_text
    Match HUD Words
    • Text

    The mode's words in the match HUD in place of the zone's timer (empty: the zone again).

    The text. Leave it empty to use the setting.
