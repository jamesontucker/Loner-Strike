    Knocked Down On

    actionkd.enable
    Knocked Down On

    On: a hit that would kill a player (of a team, or anyone) knocks them down instead: they crawl and bleed out over some seconds; a teammate (a squad mate, with squads) holding USE for 4 s gets them up with 50 health. Off: nobody new goes down.

    Inputs (1)
    Run Flow
    Runs this node.
    Outputs (1)
    Then Flow
    Goes on when it's done.
    Settings (4)
    On
    On or off. Default: true.
    Bleed out after (seconds)
    A number from 3 to 300. Default: 30.
    Who
    Pick one: Anyone, Terrorists, Counter-Terrorists. Default: 0.
    They lose their perks
    On or off. Default: true.
    Notes (1)

    Needs Loner-Strike's components plugin, which comes with the app.
