    Players Pass Through Each Other

    actioncol.semiclip
    Players Pass Through Each Other
    • Player (chosen players)

    Lets players walk through each other (semiclip): nobody, everyone, teammates only, or chosen players (each wired player set on or off; a chosen player passes through everyone). Bullets still hit. Turning it off nudges anyone left inside someone else free. Bots path as usual.

    Inputs (2)
    Run Flow
    Runs this node.
    Player (chosen players) Player
    Player (chosen players): a player. Optional.
    Outputs (1)
    Then Flow
    Goes on when it's done.
    Settings (2)
    Who
    Pick one: Nobody (off), Everyone, Teammates only, Chosen players. Default: all.
    This player passes through (chosen players)
    On or off. Default: true.

    Spawn Trigger Volume

    actionvol.spawn
    Spawn Trigger Volume
    • Position
    • Rotation
    • At entity
    • Local offset
    • Spawned
    • Failed
    • Entity
    • Success
    • Failure reason

    An invisible box or sphere that tells you who goes in and out (Begin Overlap, End Overlap). Where: a Position, or At entity plus a Local offset (X forward, Y right, Z up by its facing) - attached, it follows the entity and turns with it. Rotation (pitch, yaw, roll) turns a box; a sphere ignores it. Size is its own setting; Velocity and Scale don't apply (a volume doesn't move by itself). Who counts: players, creatures or anything. Checked ten times a second (one search round each volume). A name lets Player Touched and the overlap events pick it out. Then runs right away whatever happened; Spawned or Failed says which, and Failure reason why (Out of entity slots, Limit reached: Trigger volumes at once in Limits, or Bad input: At entity is gone and there's no Position). It isn't solid, so it never checks the spot.

    Inputs (5)
    Run Flow
    Runs this node.
    Position Vector
    The position.
    Rotation Vector
    Rotation: a position or direction (X, Y, Z). Optional.
    At entity Entity
    At entity: an entity (a thing in the map). Optional.
    Local offset Vector
    Local offset: a position or direction (X, Y, Z). Optional.
    Outputs (6)
    Then Flow
    Goes on when it's done.
    Spawned Flow
    Runs on Spawned.
    Failed Flow
    Runs on Failed.
    Entity Trigger Volume
    Entity: volume.
    Success Yes/No
    Success: yes or no.
    Failure reason ls_spawn_fail
    Failure reason: enum:ls_spawn_fail.
    Settings (7)
    Shape
    Pick one: Box, Sphere. Default: box.
    Width (box)
    A number from 8 to 8192. Default: 128.
    Depth (box)
    A number from 8 to 8192. Default: 128.
    Height (box)
    A number from 8 to 8192. Default: 96.
    Radius (sphere)
    A number from 8 to 4096. Default: 96.
    Who counts
    Pick one: Players, Creatures, Anything. Default: players.
    Name
    A name, as the map or the game knows it (letters, digits, _ . -).
    Limits (2)
    Trigger volumes at once
    32 by default; hosts can raise it to 256. Above 64 the server may slow down.
    Things in one trigger volume
    32 by default; hosts can raise it to 64. Above 48 the server may slow down.
