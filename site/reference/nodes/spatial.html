    Players In Radius

    valuespatial.players_in_radius
    Players In Radius
    • Around point
    • Around entity
    • Radius
    • Players

    Every player within Radius of a point or an entity, as an array (an entity wired in isn't counted itself).

    Inputs (3)
    Around point Vector
    Around point: a position or direction (X, Y, Z). Optional.
    Around entity Entity
    Around entity: an entity (a thing in the map).
    Radius Number
    Radius: a number. Leave it empty to use the setting.
    Outputs (1)
    Players Array of Player
    Players: a list of players.
    Settings (3)
    Radius
    A number from 1 to 8192. A wire into the pin of the same name is used instead. Default: 300.
    Alive only
    On or off. Default: true.
    Team
    Pick one: Any team, Terrorists, Counter-Terrorists. Default: any.

    Nearest Player

    valuespatial.nearest_player
    Nearest Player
    • Around point
    • Around entity
    • Player
    • Distance

    The player nearest to a point or an entity (not the entity itself), within Max distance; nobody if none.

    Inputs (2)
    Around point Vector
    Around point: a position or direction (X, Y, Z). Optional.
    Around entity Entity
    Around entity: an entity (a thing in the map).
    Outputs (2)
    Player Player
    The player.
    Distance Number
    Distance: a number.
    Settings (4)
    Max distance
    A number from 1 to 16384. Default: 8192.
    Alive only
    On or off. Default: true.
    Team
    Pick one: Any team, Terrorists, Counter-Terrorists. Default: any.
    Who
    Pick one: Bots and people, People only, Bots only. Default: all.
    Related:Players In RadiusEntities In RadiusNearest Entity

    Entities In Radius

    valuespatial.entities_in_radius
    Entities In Radius
    • Around point
    • Around entity
    • Radius
    • Entities

    Entities within Radius of a point or an entity, of a class or any, as an array (up to the mode's Limits setting, 64 at first). Players aren't listed (use Players In Radius).

    Inputs (3)
    Around point Vector
    Around point: a position or direction (X, Y, Z). Optional.
    Around entity Entity
    Around entity: an entity (a thing in the map).
    Radius Number
    Radius: a number. Leave it empty to use the setting.
    Outputs (1)
    Entities Array of Entity
    Entities: a list of entity (thing in the map)s.
    Settings (2)
    Radius
    A number from 1 to 32768. A wire into the pin of the same name is used instead. Default: 300.
    Class (empty: any)
    A name, as the map or the game knows it (letters, digits, _ . -).
    Limits (1)
    Entities one search keeps
    64 by default; hosts can raise it to 2048. Above 256 the server may slow down.
    Related:Nearest PlayerNearest EntityPlayers In Radius

    Line of Sight

    valuespatial.line_of_sight
    Line of Sight
    • A
    • Or point A
    • B
    • Or point B
    • Clear
    • Hit position

    Is there a clear line between A and B (each an entity or a point)? Entities wired in don't block it (a player's eyes are used). Hit Position is where something got in the way.

    Inputs (4)
    A Entity
    A: an entity (a thing in the map).
    Or point A Vector
    Or point A: a position or direction (X, Y, Z). Optional.
    B Entity
    B: an entity (a thing in the map).
    Or point B Vector
    Or point B: a position or direction (X, Y, Z). Optional.
    Outputs (2)
    Clear Yes/No
    Clear: yes or no.
    Hit position Vector
    Hit position: a position or direction (X, Y, Z).
    Settings (1)
    Creatures and players block
    On or off. Default: false.
    Related:Nearest EntityEntities In RadiusNearest Player

    Find Free Spot Near

    valuespatial.free_spot
    Find Free Spot Near
    • Near
    • Found
    • Position

    A spot near a point where a player (or the hull you pick) fits: the point itself if it's free, else the nearest free one in rings round it, up to Max distance (at most the mode's Limits setting of tries, 64 at first). Found is no when there's none (Position is then the point).

    Inputs (1)
    Near Vector
    Near: a position or direction (X, Y, Z). Needs a wire.
    Outputs (2)
    Found Yes/No
    Found: yes or no.
    Position Vector
    The position.
    Settings (2)
    Size
    Pick one: Player standing, Player ducking, Large (big creatures), A point. Default: stand.
    Max distance
    A number from 0 to 4096. Default: 256.
    Limits (1)
    Tries finding a free spot
    64 by default; hosts can raise it to 1024. Above 256 the server may slow down.
    Related:Line of SightNearest EntityEntities In Radius

    Made from the app's own catalog (nodes.json, nodehelp.json, limits.ts), so it matches the app.
