    Loner-Strike: Loot

    3 entities the map editor places. Pick one for its keys and flags.

    componentls_loot_containerA crate players hold USE on to open; it gives a few items from a loot table.componentls_supply_dropWhere a supply crate can fall from the sky, with a beacon.componentls_weapon_spawnerA weapon or item lying on the floor that comes back after it's taken.

    ls_loot_container

    componentLoot crate

    A crate players hold USE on to open; it gives a few items from a loot table.

    A point entity. Needs Loner-Strike's components plugin, which comes with the app.

    Keys (9)
    Name targetname
    What map logic and modes call this component. A button whose target is this name sets it off.
    On at round start enabled
    Off: it does nothing until a mode or map logic switches it on. Default: 1. Choices: 1 Yes, 0 No.
    Sets off target
    What to set off when its main thing happens (taken, opened, extracted, broken...).
    Side friendly
    On the players' side: shoots creatures only, and creatures attack it (a turret the players own).
    Tier tier
    How good the loot is. Also its glow color. Default: 1. Choices: 1 1 - common (gray), 2 2 - good (green), 3 3 - rare (blue), 4 4 - epic (purple), 5 5 - best (gold).
    Loot table table
    A [table] in configs/gamemodes/loot.ini. Default: default.
    Seconds to open open_secs
    How long a player holds E to open it (seconds). Default: 2.
    Refills after respawn_secs
    Seconds (0 = not this round). Default: 0.
    Model model
    Empty: a weapon box.

    ls_supply_drop

    componentSupply drop

    Where a supply crate can fall from the sky, with a beacon. Players hold USE on it to open it.

    A point entity. Needs Loner-Strike's components plugin, which comes with the app.

    Keys (9)
    Name targetname
    What map logic and modes call this component. A button whose target is this name sets it off.
    On at round start enabled
    Off: it does nothing until a mode or map logic switches it on. Default: 1. Choices: 1 Yes, 0 No.
    Sets off target
    What to set off when its main thing happens (taken, opened, extracted, broken...).
    Side friendly
    On the players' side: shoots creatures only, and creatures attack it (a turret the players own).
    Tier tier
    How good the loot is. Also its glow color. Default: 1. Choices: 1 1 - common (gray), 2 2 - good (green), 3 3 - rare (blue), 4 4 - epic (purple), 5 5 - best (gold).
    Falls after at
    Seconds after the round starts (-1 = when a mode or button calls it). Default: -1.
    Seconds falling fall_secs
    How long the crate falls before it lands (seconds). Default: 20.
    Items items
    How many rolls it gives from its table. Default: 4.
    Beacon beacon
    A red beam above it until it's opened. Default: 1. Choices: 1 Yes.

    ls_weapon_spawner

    componentWeapon spawner

    A weapon or item lying on the floor that comes back after it's taken. Walk over it to pick it up.

    A point entity. Needs Loner-Strike's components plugin, which comes with the app.

    Keys (6)
    Name targetname
    What map logic and modes call this component. A button whose target is this name sets it off.
    On at round start enabled
    Off: it does nothing until a mode or map logic switches it on. Default: 1. Choices: 1 Yes, 0 No.
    Side friendly
    On the players' side: shoots creatures only, and creatures attack it (a turret the players own).
    Tier tier
    How good the loot is. Also its glow color. Default: 1. Choices: 1 1 - common (gray), 2 2 - good (green), 3 3 - rare (blue), 4 4 - epic (purple), 5 5 - best (gold).
    Item item
    ak47, deagle, vesthelm, he, hp:50, ammo ... or table:<name> to roll from a loot table. Default: table:default.
    Once per round once
    The weapon comes once per round, not again after it's taken. Default: 0. Choices: 0 No.

    Made from the app's own catalog (cstrike.fgd, lonerstrike.fgd, components.json), so it matches the app.
