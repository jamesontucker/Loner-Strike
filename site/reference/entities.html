button_target #

A target to shoot: each hit switches it on or off, sets off its target as On or Off, and flips its texture (use a +0 / +a texture pair).

A brush entity (tie brushes to it).

Keys

Name targetname
What other entities call this one when they set it off.
Sets off target
The name of what this sets off.
Draw mode rendermode
How it's drawn: Normal is solid; Texture and Additive let you see through it. Default: 0. Choices: 0 Normal, 1 Color, 2 Texture, 3 Glow, 4 Solid (cut-out blue), 5 Additive.
See-through amount renderamt
0 is invisible, 255 is fully drawn (for modes other than Normal). Default: 255.
Effect renderfx
A moving effect on top of the draw mode. Default: 0. Choices: 0 None, 1 Slow pulse, 2 Fast pulse, 3 Slow wide pulse, 4 Fast wide pulse, 9 Slow strobe, 10 Fast strobe, 11 Faster strobe, 12 Slow flicker, 13 Fast flicker, 14 Constant glow, 15 Distort, 16 Hologram.

How the compiler lights it and whether it casts shadows. Default: 0. Choices: 0 Normal, 1 Embedded fix, 2 Casts shadows (opaque), 3 Opaque and embedded fix, 6 Opaque, no light through.
Light from light_origin
An info_target to light this brush as if it were there (for moving parts).
