    Pin types

    Every pin's type, what it plugs into and how to cast to it, and the app's own enums (Entity Kind, Component Kind, Spawn Failure, Who, Weapon, Hit Zone, Damage Kind): Pin types.

    Starter library

    Ready-made pieces to add from Game Mode Studio's My library.

    Run Dialog

    macroNPCs

    A conversation from the Dialog table: line after line, each reply going to its Next row, until end. Fill the table's rows; wire Talked To NPC in.

    Inputs and outputs

    In: In, NPC, Player, Start row. Out: Ended, Last row.

    Ability with Cooldown

    macroStarter: abilities

    A template for any ability: Use runs when it's ready, Not ready says the seconds left; Bot Wants Ability for bots.

    Inputs and outputs

    In: In, Player, Cooldown. Out: Use, Not ready, Seconds left.

    Capture Zone

    functionStarter: rounds and scoring

    A capture point at an entity that a team takes by standing in it for 10 seconds; bots are sent to take it.

    Inputs and outputs

    In: At. Out: Zone.

    Countdown HUD

    macroStarter: rounds and scoring

    Counts down Seconds (0: 60) on everyone's HUD as minutes:seconds, then Done.

    Inputs and outputs

    In: In, Seconds, Label. Out: Started, Done.

    Dash

    macroStarter: movement

    A quick dash the way the player looks (Speed 0: 700), at most once every Cooldown seconds (0: 3).

    Inputs and outputs

    In: In, Player, Speed, Cooldown. Out: Dashed, Not ready, Seconds left.

    Double Jump

    macroStarter: movement

    A second jump in the air (Boost 0: 320 up), once until the player lands again.

    Inputs and outputs

    In: In, Player, Boost. Out: Jumped, Not now.

    Fireball

    macroStarter: abilities

    Throws a fireball where the player aims; it explodes where it hits (Damage 0: 60, Speed 0: 1100).

    Inputs and outputs

    In: In, Player, Damage, Speed. Out: Thrown.

    Health Pack

    macroStarter: pickups

    A health pack on a trigger volume: heals (0: 50, up to 100) and comes back after Respawn seconds (0: 20).

    Inputs and outputs

    In: In, Volume, Who, Heal, Respawn after. Out: Taken, Not taken.

    Hot Potato

    functionStarter: spectacle

    A bomb one player holds for Seconds (0: 20): a hit passes it on, and when time runs out it blows up its holder.

    Inputs and outputs

    In: Player, Seconds. Out: .

    Jump Pad

    functionStarter: movement

    Launches a player up (Speed, 0: 800) with a sound: call it from a pad's Begin Overlap.

    Inputs and outputs

    In: Who, Speed. Out: .

    Kill Cam

    macroStarter: spectacle

    Shows the victim the killer for Seconds (0: 3), from behind the killer, then back.

    Inputs and outputs

    In: In, Victim, Killer, Seconds. Out: Then.

    Kill Streak Announcer

    functionStarter: rounds and scoring

    Counts kills in a row per player and announces 3, 5, 7 and 10 to everyone; a death resets it.

    Inputs and outputs

    In: Killer, Victim. Out: Streak.

    Leaderboard Display

    functionStarter: rounds and scoring

    Shows a player the top players of a saved score (Key, e.g. kills) in an info window.

    Inputs and outputs

    In: Player. Out: .

    Lives Counter

    functionStarter: player state

    Each player's lives: Lose a Life on a death (with a HUD line), Out when none are left; Reset Lives at the start (0: 3).

    Inputs and outputs

    In: Player. Out: Lives left, Out.

    Proximity Mine

    functionStarter: abilities

    Places a mine at the player's feet that blows up when someone steps near (Radius 0: 96, Damage 0: 120).

    Inputs and outputs

    In: Player, Radius, Damage. Out: Mine.

    Quest Step Tracker

    functionStarter: story

    Each player's quest step (a word you choose, e.g. find_fuse) with a HUD line; pairs with Run Dialog's Last row.

    Inputs and outputs

    In: Player, Step, Objective. Out: .

    Spawn Protection

    macroStarter: player state

    God mode and a glow for Seconds (0: 3) after a player spawns; it ends early if they shoot.

    Inputs and outputs

    In: In, Player, Seconds. Out: Then, Over.

    Burn, Slow and Freeze

    macroStarter: player state

    Status effects with timers: Burn (Seconds 0: 4, Damage a second 0: 5), Slow (0: 4 s at 0: 150 speed) and Freeze (0: 2 s).

    Inputs and outputs

    In: In, Player, By, Seconds, Damage a second. Out: Then.

    Teleporter Pair

    functionStarter: movement

    Sends a player to a destination entity, at most once every Cooldown seconds (0: 2).

    Inputs and outputs

    In: Who, To, Cooldown. Out: Went.

    Victory Camera

    macroStarter: spectacle

    Everyone looks at the winner for Seconds (0: 5) at the end of a round.

    Inputs and outputs

    In: In, Winner, Seconds. Out: Then.

    Weapon Pickup

    macroStarter: pickups

    A weapon lying on a trigger volume: gives it (Weapon, e.g. awp) and comes back after Respawn seconds (0: 20).

    Inputs and outputs

    In: In, Volume, Who, Weapon, Respawn after. Out: Taken, Not taken.

    Made from the app's own catalog (nodes.json, nodehelp.json, limits.ts), so it matches the app.
