Privacy

Last updated November 6, 2026

Loner-Strike is made by Nanoshrine Interactive LLC ("we"). This explains what this website and the Loner-Strike app do with information about you. Short version: we don't use cookies, we don't track you, and we don't sell anything about you.

This website

  • Visitor statistics. We count visits with Plausible Analytics, which we run on our own server. It uses no cookies and stores no personal data: no IP addresses, no identifiers that follow you around. We see totals like how many people visited, which pages, which country, and which buttons were clicked. That's why there's no cookie banner.
  • Hosting. The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles your browser's requests, which include your IP address, to deliver the page and protect it from attacks. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Checkout script. The "Name your price" button loads Polar's checkout script from jsDelivr, a public code CDN, which sees your IP address when it sends the file. See jsDelivr's privacy policy.
  • Discord. After the page loads, it asks discord.com for the Discord invite and how many people are online, so Discord sees that request under its own privacy policy.
  • Radio. The optional radio player streams from Bassdrive or SomaFM when you press play, and while it plays asks the station what's on every 20 seconds, so those stations see your IP address like any listener.
  • Fonts and images are served from this site itself, not from Google or anyone else.

Paying

Paying is optional. If you do, the payment is handled by Polar, which acts as the merchant of record: Polar processes your payment, sends your receipt and handles sales tax. Polar shares your name, email address, country and what you paid with us, so we can help if something goes wrong. We never see your card details. See Polar's privacy policy.

The Loner-Strike app

The app runs on your computer and sends nothing about you to us. There's no account and no telemetry. It only goes online to:

  • check GitHub for updates, and download the mods you install from their original sources;
  • browse and download maps and other content from GameBanana, when you use those pages;
  • connect to services you choose to use for playing with friends, such as playit.gg, Tailscale, your router (UPnP) or Valve's server downloader;
  • connect over SSH to Linux servers you rent, when you add one.

Each of those services has its own privacy policy. Your settings, admin lists and the list of players who joined your games stay on your computer, and on the servers you run.

Your rights

If you paid and want a copy of what we hold about you, or want it deleted, ask us and we'll take care of it. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you have rights over your personal data, and you can contact your local data protection authority.
