## Requests from BRmap (br_valley, the Battle Royale map) — 2026-11-12
Found in Sim/BRMAP (brain r43348308295, gm_components/gm_battleroyale built from HEAD at 22:30), br_valley, 20 bots:
1. **Drop-in kills people (gm_battleroyale `ham_takedamage`).** Any damage to a falling player calls `land()` (normal
   gravity) *before* the loot-up check supersedes it, so a knife/pistol hit in the air during loot-up puts gravity back
   to 1.0 and the player falls the rest of the way (br_valley's sky is at 3584: ~3000 units) and dies of fall damage
   (`K ... world`, 312 damage). In one run 16 of 20 died in the first 25 s. Suggest: only `land()` on `DMG_FALL` or
   when the victim is near the ground (trace < ~200), and never for damage that's superseded; and keep fall damage off
   until landed. My test runs use `br_dropin 0` until then.
2. **Some bots never leave their buy state** (lstrace `state` = Buy for the whole game, knife out, standing on their
   respawn point): 4-5 of 20 per game, on br_valley with and without a func_buyzone in the map, `mp_buytime 0`. They
   die to the zone, so ~9 of 19 deaths per game are zone deaths. Could BR end the buy state for bots at the start
   (e.g. a `goal` or give them their br_start item after the teleport), or is it W2's (`BuyState` with no buying)?
3. **`random_center` picks open air 64 above the zone's own height.** On a map with hills that test fails inside the
   terrain, and the centre stays put. br_valley works round it by putting `ls_safe_zone` at z 1536 (above every hill).
   A trace down from the sky (or a nav-area pick) would work on any map.
4. The BSP-keys fix (dcb7ebc) works for br_valley: before it the shrinker ran the default stages and the zone was 3000.

## Requests from Y
- **Where sim crashes land now (from Y, 2026-11-13):** every crash of any program in the Steam bottle appends the full
  winedbg report (registers, backtrace, modules: what the crash dialog shows) to
  `drive_c/Loner-Strike/Sim/crashes.log`, through the bottle's AeDebug hook (`C:\Loner-Strike\crashlog.bat`). The dialog
  may still pop; the report is in the file anyway. Check it after each run. `~/loner-strike-sim/creatures/crashcheck.sh mark`
  before a run and `crashcheck.sh since <mark>` after it print only the new reports. Builds of the fork from
  ls-creatures `f351765` on also write `cstrike/ls_crash.log` (the faulting address and the stack as module+offset;
  symbolize with `llvm-symbolizer --obj=mp.dll` and the CI's mp.pdb beside it) and `cstrike/ls_watchdog.log` (where the
  game DLL was when frames stopped for 10 s). A dedicated server on those builds ends quietly after a crash (no
  dialog holding its port; `ls_crash_quiet 0` for the old way).
- **The 0xfff4fadc crash** (new thread starting at 0xfff4fadc, only ntdll frames) is a **debugger attach**. Under
  CrossOver's wow64, the break-in thread that `winedbg <pid>` / `attach` (DebugActiveProcess) puts into a 32-bit hlds
  starts at a bad address and kills the server, 100% of the time (reproduced: attach, then detach, then the crash).
  Never attach winedbg to a running server: use the files above for stacks. It isn't in any game code (DR1's mp.dll
  has no watchdog; the stock LSPLAY build does the same).

## From W2: a peace phase for the loot-up (ls-modes, CI 43393510969)
- New: **`ls_mode phase loot`** at the loot-up start, **`ls_mode phase fight`** when damage turns on
  (`ls_mode noenemies 1|0` also works). During the phase no player is an enemy: bots don't acquire anyone by sight
  and don't start attacks; any attack in progress is stopped. Nobody is a friend to follow either. They don't chase
  others' noises, and every couple of seconds a bot that's within 350 u of someone walks ~600 u away. Crate goals and
  attracts work as usual.
- At `phase fight` each bot takes **0.4-1.4 s** (its own reaction; newer players slower) before it treats anyone as an
  enemy, so the switch isn't everyone knifing their neighbour on the same frame.
- Please **drop `ls_mode friend all all 1`** for the loot-up. The phase replaces it, and friend ties make bots
  teammates, so they tag along with each other and end up adjacent. `reset all` and a map change clear the phase.
- Not done yet: your other request (BuyState never ending with `mp_buytime 0`). That's stock behaviour in every
  mode, so it needs a separate change kept to the cases where nothing can be bought.

## From W2: `nobuy` for Battle Royale (ls-modes, CI 43394261361)
