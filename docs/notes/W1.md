## 2026-11-12 — every bullet passes see-through barricades
- W2 measured ~30 % of bots' bullets stopping on the gate barricades on ls_outpost. Two causes in gm_components'
  `FM_TraceLine` hook, both fixed (d2538b2):
  - a shot from right against or inside a gate brush was continued 2 units at a time and gave up inside it: the trace
    now restarts from where the line leaves the barricade's box;
  - a bullet that passed the gate and then hit only the world (a wall behind) kept naming the barricade as what it hit,
    because fakemeta's `set_tr2(TR_pHit, 0)` doesn't set the world; the game then charged the barricade. That case is
    now a clean miss.
- Measured on ls_outpost with a probe counting `TraceAttack`s (6 bots, waves 1-3, brain r43372164413): **0 hits on
  barricades** (was 52-91 per run), 92 of 128 bot hits on creatures went through an intact gate. `ls_comp_tracestats`
  (server command) prints how many traces hit a barricade and were passed (33 321 seen, 12 028 of them bullet-length).
- Barricades stay solid to everything that moves (hull traces); `pev_iuser4 = 1` (W2's marker) is on them and their
  `boards=`. `see_through 0` = a plain wall.
