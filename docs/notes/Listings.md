### Title
Loner-Strike: Human-like bots, map & mode tools, and servers for CS 1.6 + CZ

### Summary (short, for the page header)
Play Counter-Strike like it's 2004, alone or with the friends you don't have. Human-like bots, a map editor, a game mode builder, your own servers and a community browser, in one free app for CS 1.6 and Condition Zero.

### Full description
Play Counter-Strike like it's 2004, alone or with the friends you don't have.

Loner-Strike is a free, open-source (GPL-3.0) app for Counter-Strike 1.6 and Condition Zero on Steam. It adds to the game you already own. Everything it changes is backed up, and you can remove it from the app.

**Bots that play like people**
- Our own bot brain, built on ReGameDLL. Bots study every map, see only what you could, and decide for themselves: hold, re-peek, flank or fall back.
- They aim like a hand on a mouse, react like people, and play as a team.
- They chat, banter and rage, and they answer you in team chat or on your mic ("rush b", "flash for me", "someone buy me an awp").
- About 1,600 regulars with personalities and clan tags who remember you across days.
- Ten lobbies, from a newbie lobby to Gods. You can also keep the stock bots.

**Modes and maps**
- 22 classic community modes, installed and patched so bots play them: Zombie Plague, Warcraft 3, SuperHero, Gun Game, Deathmatch, Hide and Seek, Deathrun, Surf, Kreedz, Base Builder, SoccerJam, Paintball and more.
- Two modes made here:
  - **Last Stand**: co-op defense against waves of creatures, on ls_outpost (best for 4 to 16 players).
  - **Battle Royale**: drop in, loot up and outlast the zone, on br_valley.
- 27 maps installed for you, and thousands more from GameBanana, with screenshots and search.

**Make your own (Beta)**
- **Map editor**: Hammer's depth with a modern editor feel, with real brushes and entities in 3D.
  - A Content Browser, live checks, and SDHLT compiling.
  - Press F5 to playtest with bots.
  - Opens Hammer .rmf, J.A.C.K. .jmf and .map files.
- **Game Mode Studio**: game modes from nodes and wires, debugged live in the game.
- **Plugin Studio** for AMX Mod X, and **Creature Studio** for creatures and waves.
- **Map tools**:
  - Map simulator: bots play a match on your map, and the report is drawn on it.
  - Map doctor, nav fixer, callouts and radar maker.
- **Personalize**: Spray maker, Crosshair & config, and Bot people.

**Host**
- Your friends only need the game. There are seven ways to connect, from a LAN party to playit.gg, Tailscale or Hamachi.
- Dedicated servers on your computer or a rented Linux machine, set up for you.
- Admins, moderators and helpers who run it from their own app.
- Bans, mutes, votes, 5v5 matches, HLTV and daily backups.
- Control it all from your phone.

**Share**
- The Community browser has maps, Studio modes, prefabs and sprays made with Loner-Strike, hosted on GameBanana. Press Try it and play with bots.
- Package your work in the app and upload it to GameBanana, with the credits filled in for you. It then shows up in Community.
- Remixes credit the original, and updates arrive between maps.
- Nothing that runs code is shared: a Studio mode travels as its graph, and each player's app builds it.

**Requirements**
- Counter-Strike 1.6 or Condition Zero on Steam.
- Windows 10 or 11, or a Mac with the Windows version of Steam in CrossOver.

Free download, or name your price at https://lonerstrike.com. Source code: https://github.com/jamesontucker/Loner-Strike

### News article: "Loner-Strike 1.0 is out"

**Loner-Strike 1.0 is out**

Loner-Strike started as a way to make late-night Counter-Strike against bots fun again: bots that feel like the regulars on an old community server, and the classic mods patched so they play them too. Version 1.0 is the full release. Now it covers the whole loop: build something, play it with bots, host it and share it.

**What's new in 1.0**
- **Two modes made here.**
  - Last Stand: co-op defense against waves of creatures on ls_outpost, endless by default, with barricades, revives, an armory and supply drops.
  - Battle Royale: a drop-in, loot-up, shrinking-zone mode on br_valley.
  - Bots play both, and both also work on several stock maps.
- **Tools (Beta).**
  - A map editor with Hammer's depth and a modern editor feel: a Content Browser, live checks, SDHLT compiling, and F5 to playtest with bots. It opens Hammer and J.A.C.K. maps.
  - Game Mode Studio, for game modes from nodes and wires.
  - Plugin Studio and Creature Studio.
  - A map simulator, map doctor, nav fixer, callouts, radar maker, spray maker and more.
- **Community.** A browser for maps, modes, prefabs and sprays made with Loner-Strike, hosted on GameBanana and moderated.
  - Try anything with bots in a click.
  - Package your own in the app and upload it to GameBanana, with the credits filled in for you.
  - Remixes credit the original, and updates arrive between maps, never mid-game.
- **Servers.**
  - Servers update themselves once nobody's playing.
  - A Linux server is backed up every day.
  - New Health, Who can join, Moderation and Stats tabs.
  - Safer shared control.

Everything from before is still here: the bot brain, 22 classic modes, seven ways for friends to connect, and dedicated servers on your computer or a rented Linux machine.

It's free and open source. Get it at https://lonerstrike.com, read the docs at https://lonerstrike.com/docs, and tell us what you think on Discord: https://lonerstrike.com/discord
