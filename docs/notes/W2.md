## Rule from Y (2026-11-13): touch only your own server processes
- Never attach a debugger (`winedbg <pid>`, `attach`, DebugBreakProcess, anything that puts a thread into another
  process) to a running hlds under CrossOver: the injected thread starts at 0xfff4fadc and kills the server.
- Stop, signal or inspect only the processes your own script started: by the PID you got when you started it, or by a
  pattern that names your own sim folder (`Sim[\\/]<yours>[\\/]hlds.exe`). Never by name (`hlds.exe`, `winedbg`) and
  never by "the newest process". Before a start, make sure your port is free (`lsof -nP -iUDP:<port>`).
- Stacks and crashes without a debugger: `drive_c/Loner-Strike/Sim/crashes.log` (every crash in the bottle, full
  winedbg report), and with fork builds from ls-creatures `f351765` on, `cstrike/ls_crash*.log` and
  `cstrike/ls_watchdog.log` in the server folder.

#### Personality influence on mode orders (protocol, ls_mode_ver 2)
An optional **influence 0-100** on each order says how much the bot's own personality and skill decide whether,
when and how it follows it. **Not given = today's behavior** (the usual mix, the same as before this argument).
0 = always, exactly as given; 100 = the personality fully decides (each personal part doubled; a careful bot may turn
down a risky goal, a low-skill one is slower and less tidy about it). Values in between scale it (50 = not given).
| Order | Where the influence goes | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| `goal <who> <kind> x y z [ent] [prio] [until] [radius] [influence]` | 11th arg | taking it up (teamwork for team jobs, greed for loot/use, caution for flee; risk at the spot - creatures within 600, enemy players within 800 - puts the careful off; prio 80+ is always taken unless influence > 50), the start delay (lower skill reacts later: up to ~2.4 s at 100, none at 0 or prio 90), hold times at a station (careful longer), pushing out from a defend (bold more; never at 0), tagging along with a person between waves, and a revive's risk tolerance (0: goes in regardless, like prio 90; 100: careful takes no risk, the boldest up to three threats) |
| `offer <who> <kind> x y z ent cost [order] [influence]` | 10th arg | money kept over the price (200 at 0; careful up to 500, bold 0 at 100), bench vs turret order (gun players - bold, skilled - bench first; careful team players turret first; at 0 the fixed order perks, bench, turret, box), the box roll (greed), the trap's pressure threshold (5 at 0; team players and careful sooner, loners later) |
| `phase <loot\|fight\|off> [influence]` | 3rd arg | how long each bot takes to notice the fight is on (0: at once; up to ~2.8 s for slow, low-skill bots at 100), how long an unarmed bot keeps gearing up (80 s at 0; bold give up sooner, careful look longer) |
| `layer <who> <below\|surface\|high\|-> [influence]` | 4th arg | how strongly its looting keeps to the told layer (0: almost only it; 50/not given: as before; 100: some bots go by their own taste instead) |
| `ls_bot <id> follow <leader> [secs] [influence]` | 5th arg | above 50, a loner may turn the follow down (not taken: gm_botchat sees no fuser4 stamp) |
| `ls_bot <id> goto x y z [walk] [hold] [bc] [influence]` | 9th arg | above 50, a loner, or a careful bot sent somewhere it remembers as dangerous, may turn it down |
Also new hints the brain now acts on (gm_components already sends them): `heals <who> <bandages> <medkits> <drinks>`
and `heal <who> <kind|-> [secs]` (stands still while healing). `ls_mode status` shows each goal's influence.
