## Requests from W1
- **BuyState never ends when nothing can be bought** (Battle Royale: `mp_buytime 0`, $0, knife start). On br_valley 4-5 of
  20 bots stood on their respawn point in `Buy` for the whole game (26-28 % of bot samples, BRmap's runs) and died to
  the zone. In `BuyState::OnUpdate`, `m_doneBuying` only ends the state outside the freeze period and the buy attempts
  with no money/time just retry. Could it end at once (Idle) when `CanPlayerBuy` is false / buy time is over / the
  player has no money for anything, and never re-enter it mid-round? gm_battleroyale works round it for now: a bot that
  hasn't moved 8 s into the game gets `ls_bot <i> goto <a nearby crate>` (up to 3 tries) and `release` 12 s later.
