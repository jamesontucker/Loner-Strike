From user feedback on three tools. All checked at 1440×900 and 1024×680 on tools-dev.html (my ports 8047/5183).

## Spray maker → spray composer (`views/tools/spray/`, `tools/spray.rs`)
- **Layers** (`doc.ts`): pictures (file input, several at once, kept as PNG ≤1024 px), words (8 fonts: the app's
  Counter-Strike font + system-safe ones; bold/italic, colour, outline, shadow), 18 original stickers
  (`stickers.ts`: splat, skull, star, burst, heart, bolt, flame, crown, shield, crosshair, circle, ring, square,
  diamond, triangle, arrow, speech bubble, banner). Each: move, turn, scale, flip, opacity, colour; pictures can clear
  their corner colour and be coloured in; stickers can be stretched and outlined.
- **Canvas** (`Stage.tsx`): Square / Wide / Tall (512 design units on the long side = the game's 96×96, 128×96,
  96×128 at the largest size), see-through (checkerboard) or a colour. Handles: drag to move, corners to scale, the dot
  to turn. Snapping (on by default, Alt ignores): canvas middle and edges, other layers' middles (guide lines), turns
  in 15°. Shift = straight line.
- **Layer list** (`Panels.tsx`): top first, hide, lock, up/down, duplicate, delete. Keys: Delete, Mod+D, [ ], arrows
  (Shift 10), Esc, Mod+Z / Shift+Mod+Z / Ctrl+Y (`history.ts`: a drag is one step, sliders merge). In the `?` sheet.
- **In game**: the design is drawn on the page, sent as a PNG (`POST /api/tools/spray/use`), then the existing
  `/preview` and `/install` run unchanged (same palette reduction, sizes, backups, put back). The preview shows the
  real result on Plaster (CSS) or the game's own `cs_dust.wad` textures (Sandstone, Crate) at texture scale, 1-3×, plus
  real size. Options: size, full / one colour (+ paint, invert), smooth colours, games, read-only.
- **Templates** (`templates.ts`): Blank, Name tag, Skull banner, Team badge, Headshot, GG.
- **Designs**: saved as JSON in `<app>/sprays/designs/<id>.json` with a thumbnail; Designs & templates sheet opens or
  deletes them. Routes: `GET /api/tools/spray/designs`, `GET /design?id=`, `POST /design/save`, `POST /design/delete`.
  After the security review: a page id is only taken to update a design that exists; new ids come from the server; caps
  200 designs, 24 MB each, 256 MB in all. MCP: `/use` and `/design/save` ask agents first (routes.rs + test).
- ActionBar: Save design, More (save as new, add a picture, blank), **Install spray** as the main button.

## Rule from Y (2026-11-13): touch only your own server processes
- Never attach a debugger (`winedbg <pid>`, `attach`, DebugBreakProcess, anything that puts a thread into another
  process) to a running hlds under CrossOver: the injected thread starts at 0xfff4fadc and kills the server.
- Stop, signal or inspect only the processes your own script started: by the PID you got when you started it, or by a
  pattern that names your own sim folder (`Sim[\\/]<yours>[\\/]hlds.exe`). Never by name (`hlds.exe`, `winedbg`) and
  never by "the newest process". Before a start, make sure your port is free (`lsof -nP -iUDP:<port>`).
- Stacks and crashes without a debugger: `drive_c/Loner-Strike/Sim/crashes.log` (every crash in the bottle, full
  winedbg report), and with fork builds from ls-creatures `f351765` on, `cstrike/ls_crash*.log` and
  `cstrike/ls_watchdog.log` in the server folder.

## Spray maker: Start sheet, opacity checked in game, new templates (2026-11-13)
- **Start a spray** (`spray/StartFrom.tsx`) uses the tools' `EditorDialog` (header + ×, scrolling body, footer with
  Close). Your designs first (newest first, the open one marked, delete on hover/focus, a real empty state), then
  templates in a 4-column grid (2 under 620 px), cards one size, previews on a bit of plaster. Focus starts on the open
  design or the first template; arrow keys/Home/End move between cards; Esc closes and focus goes back to the button
  (the button focuses itself on click, since WebKit doesn't).
- **Opacity in the real game** (checked 2026-11-13, not guessed): installed test sprays through the dev server's
  `/use` + `/install`, launched hl.exe in the CrossOver Steam bottle (cstrike de_dust2, czero de_dust_cz), sprayed with
  `impulse 201` and took `snapshot`s (driven with rcon `exec` of small cfgs, menus closed with Esc/number keys).
  Test picture: solid magenta, white at 50% alpha, yellow alpha ramp (full color); black solid, mid grey, black at 50%,
  black alpha ramp (one color).
  - Full color, both games: completely opaque, no wall texture shows through; only lit by the wall's light. 50% white
    came out solid; the ramp shows only its ≥50% half, solid (wadwrite's alpha ≥ 128 cut, index 255 = clear).
  - One color, both games: real see-through: the index is coverage. Black = solid paint, grey and 50% alpha = the wall
    shows through about half, the ramp is a smooth fade.
  - So the preview's `mix-blend-mode: multiply` + `opacity: .94` was wrong (it made 100% look see-through). Now
    plain alpha over the wall, which is exactly `Spray::to_rgba` (colour: 0/255, mono: index). The Opacity field's
    help and a short line under it say what happens per mode; the Colors help says it too.
  - Not modelled: the wall's lightmap (the game darkens sprays in shade) and the game's texture smoothing.
- **Templates** (16, Blank included; each checked at 96×96 through `/preview`): Blank, Stencil tag, Graffiti tag,
  Arcade (wide), Clan emblem, Planted here, Nice try, Noob (tall), One tap (wide), Pixel heart, Pixel skull,
  Counter-Terrorist, Terrorist, Monogram, GG WP, Loner-Strike mark (last, optional). The composer starts on Graffiti tag.
- **New stickers** (original shapes): wings, crossed rifles, bomb, paint drips, spray can, laurel, chevrons, plate,
  pixel heart, pixel skull, pixel star. Stickers can set `fill: "nonzero"` (overlapping parts without holes).
- US English in the spray files (scripts/us-english-check.py --fix); data keys stay `colour`.
- Tests: p10.spec 8/8 in Chromium and in WebKit (both sizes; WebKit via a scratch config with `browserName: webkit`),
