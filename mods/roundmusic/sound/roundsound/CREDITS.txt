Round end music - seeded tracks
===============================
Drop your own .mp3 / .wav files into these folders - the roundmusic add-on finds them
automatically at the next map change (no config needed):
  t_win/    played when the Terrorists win
  ct_win/   played when the Counter-Terrorists win
  (this folder itself) played for either team
File names: no spaces. wav must be 8/16-bit PCM, mono, 11025 or 22050 Hz. mp3: plain CBR mp3.
Max 16 files per folder are used per map (a random 16 if there are more).

The seeded tracks below are short excerpts (cut to 7-14 s, faded out, loudness-normalised
to -16 LUFS, re-encoded as 112 kbps mp3). All are free licences; CC-BY tracks require the
credit below. Excerpting/fading is the only modification.
