## Unreleased

The first public version, 0.1.0.

- Setup for Counter-Strike 1.6 and Condition Zero on Windows, and on Mac through CrossOver: Metamod-P, AMX Mod X, ReGameDLL, the Game Mode Menu and the mods you pick, all backed up and removable.
- A control panel in the app and on your phone: game modes, maps, bots, rules, players, sounds and server extras.
- Bot styles with human-like aim and reactions, and bot fixes for every bundled mod.
- Mode voting: rock the vote (rtv or rtm) picks the game mode first, then a map that suits it, and every few maps the vote at the end of a map can do the same. The Rules page picks which modes are in the vote.
- Servers on your computer or on a Linux VPS over SSH, with HLTV, shared ban and admin lists, and changes synced as you make them.
- Ways to play with friends: the same network, a direct connection through your router, playit.gg, Tailscale, ZeroTier, Radmin VPN or Hamachi.
