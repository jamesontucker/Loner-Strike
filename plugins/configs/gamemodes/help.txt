GAME MODE MENU - press F3, or say /gm

 1 Presets     One-click setups. Save your own from here too.
 2 Game mode   Switch modes. The map reloads.
 3 Maps        Browse by type, random map, bot navigation,
               map presets (surf/scoutz maps pick their mode)
 4 Bots        Count, difficulty, weapons, roster, chatter
 5 Match       Round time, money, friendly fire, limits
 6 Fun         Gravity, air control, weapons, unlimited ammo,
               headshots only, bunnyhop, tracers, damage numbers,
               zombie darkness
 7 Players     God, noclip, give weapons, money, teleport, kick
 8 Add-ons     Grappling hook, Quake sounds, gore
 9 Admin       Every AMX Mod X menu, and this help

KEYS
 F3  this menu        F4  quick actions
 V   grappling hook (when the add-on is on)

PRACTICE
 F4 > Save my spot / Back to my saved spot
 Fun > Respawn me instantly, Unlimited money
 Bots > Peaceful bots (they don't move or shoot)

CHAT
 /gm  menu   zpmenu  zombie menu   /guns  deathmatch guns
 /rank /top15 /statsme  stats      /hp  who killed you

BOTS STAND STILL ON A MAP?
 The map has no bot navigation yet. Maps > Bot navigation
 > Learn this map, or let it prepare every map at once.
 Maps marked * in the browser still need it.

TOUR OF DUTY
 Mission packs are picked on CZ's Tour of Duty screen.
 Game modes pause automatically during missions.
